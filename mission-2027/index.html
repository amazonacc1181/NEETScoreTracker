03 — A note from the future

For the version of you who feels tired tonight.

Read this on the difficult days. Not to make the pressure heavier, but to remember that your past attempts do not get to decide your worth.

PERSONAL LETTER / 2027KEEP THIS CLOSE

Dear future doctor,

I know how many times you have had to begin again. I know you sometimes wonder whether one more attempt can really change your life.

But listen: you are not the same person who began the first attempt. You have seen what doesn't work. You know what fear feels like. You know how hard it is to return to a desk after a disappointing day. That experience is not wasted.

One day, you may be standing in a hospital corridor, wearing a coat you once only imagined. Maybe you will remember these quiet evenings—not every chapter, not every score, but the decision to sit down again.

Do not ask yourself to be perfect. Ask yourself to be honest, consistent and kind enough to keep going. Take breaks. Sleep. Speak to the people who care about you. Then return to the work.

Whatever the result, your value is not a rank. But while this dream is still yours, give it your most sincere effort.

With faith in you,
the person you are becoming.
